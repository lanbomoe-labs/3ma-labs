Frequently Asked Questions

Find immediate operational clarity regarding SMT 2026, 3MA development tracks, and assessment structures.

3MA Labs (3niti Multimedia Academy) is an elite educational portal and technology innovation lab dedicated to training children and teenagers (ages 4 to 19) as System Builders using a Router-First Curriculum. Under the visionary leadership of Moses Olanrewaju Bolarin, 3MA Labs combines deep technical infrastructure, AI automation, full-stack software development, and digital leadership.
A Router-First Curriculum is our proprietary educational methodology. Rather than teaching syntax in isolation, we train young minds to understand how internet data packets route across server configurations and DNS nodes. This provides young System Builders with real-world architectural power over standard closed ecosystems.
3MA (3niti Multimedia Academy) is the core active brand of the academy. MMA (Majestic Multimedia Academy) is the former or alternative branding of the academy, under which our pioneering childrens publications were released. SMT (Summer Multimedia Training) is our flagship 4-week summer intensive challenge that runs annually to accelerate students through tech competencies.
The CBT Platform manages robust standardized computer tests across our entire physical school networks and virtual academies. It auto-grades assessments, monitors performance metrics, and triggers secure digital badges and credentials verified directly by Founder Moses Olanrewaju Bolarin.
Moses Olanrewaju Bolarin is a Systems Architect, digital brand strategist, bestselling author, and CEO of 3MA Labs. He advocates a "Sovereign-First" computing philosophy: that hardware is a disposable commodity, but digital identity, secure encryption, and technological agency are permanent human assets that must be nurtured early.
Outstanding student storybooks are compiled and published globally on Amazon KDP under the 3MA Children Projects Publications initiative. Under our non-commercial Social Re-investment Covenant, 100% of royalties are directed to the 3MA Digital Equity Fund to purchase hardware for underprivileged children, while students gain elite credentials as SMT Certified Published Authors.
Students require reliable internet access, any connected smart device (such as a laptop, desktop, Chromebook, or tablet), and a custom physical journal to map data ciphers, code routes, and systemic architectures by hand.
